    About you

    Every answer changes the prompts Pepper writes. Each question says how. Answers save as you go.

    Memories

    Short facts Pepper adds to the context of every prompt while they're switched on. Keep them true and specific: "I'm targeting new-grad SWE roles in Boston."

    Suggested from your prompts

    Pepper can read prompts you've sent on sites with learning on and suggest facts you stated about yourself. Nothing becomes a memory until you add it. This sends those prompts to your model provider.

    Projects and files

    A project tells Pepper what kind of work you're doing and which files are the source of truth. Pick the active project from the panel on any AI site.

    Files

    Text files are read automatically. For PDF or Word files, paste the text once. Pepper uses up to 2,500 characters per file and 6,000 per prompt, and marks anything trimmed.

    Saved prompts

    Prompts you saved from the panel. They show up as suggestions in the panel for the same project.

    History

    Prompts you sent on sites where learning is on, with up to 4,000 characters of each reply and your rating. Kept for 90 days or 2,000 prompts.

    What Pepper learned

    From the prompts you sent through Pepper and rated, on sites where learning is on, Pepper works out which approach to pick first and how long to aim for each kind of work, and it suggests your best past requests. Your profile and memories change only when you confirm a suggestion.

    Worked best for you

    Your 4 and 5 star requests. They appear as suggestions in the panel.

    Privacy and data

    Everything Pepper keeps stays in this browser. If you add a model key, the text Pepper needs goes to that provider when you ask it to write, rewrite or find facts. Nothing else leaves.

    Learning, site by site

    When learning is on for a site, Pepper saves each prompt you send there, up to 4,000 characters of the reply, and your star rating. That's what lets it learn which approach works for you. When it's off, Pepper still writes prompts but saves nothing.

    Model and settings

    With your own API key, your model writes the prompt. Without one, Pepper uses its built-in template, which works offline but is less tailored.
